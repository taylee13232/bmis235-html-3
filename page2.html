#2 - Mac and Cheese

Mac and cheese is my second favorite Thanksgiving side dish. It is creamy, cheesy, and always a favorite at the dinner table.

I love how it goes with almost everything on the Thanksgiving menu. It is also a great comfort food that I look forward to eating.

Even though it is not my number one choice, Thanksgiving would not be the same without a big serving of mac and cheese!
