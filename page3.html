#3 - Mashed Potatoes

Mashed potatoes are my third favorite Thanksgiving side dish. They are creamy, warm, and go perfectly with turkey and gravy.

I like how simple they are, but they still add so much to the meal. They are even better with butter and gravy on top.

Mashed potatoes may be number three on my list, but they are still an important part of my Thanksgiving dinner!
